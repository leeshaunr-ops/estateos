For home watch companies caring for seasonal and unoccupied residences

A home watch visit can lead to a repair, a question for the owner, or preparation for an upcoming arrival. EstateAegis keeps those details with the home so your team can see the latest report and the work still needing attention.

Plan recurring care

Set the inspection frequency and next due date for a residence. Give your team a shared view of upcoming visits and outstanding work.

Document the visit

Record inspection findings, notes, and photos. Yellow Monitor and red Attention findings help owners see which items need a closer look.

Keep follow-up visible

Create linked work orders from published findings. Assign the work, add progress notes, and request more information or client approval.

How it works

  1. 1

  2. 2

    Save the reporting details

  4. 4

    Coordinate the response

    Use linked work orders and messages to move repairs and questions forward.

Common questions

Know what to expect.

Can the homeowner see reports?

Clients have their own portal for the residences they can access. Your team can also deliver the published inspection report to the saved email recipient.

Can reports use my company name?

Yes. Add your company name and logo in company settings. Inspection PDFs carry your company branding, with Powered by EstateAegis in the footer.

What happens when an owner is returning?

Plan the arrival in the residence record. Use room readiness, preferred shopping items, guests, and special instructions to organize the preparation.
