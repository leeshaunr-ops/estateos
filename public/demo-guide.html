Your first five minutes

  1. Activate your account. Open your invitation email and use its one-use link. Choose your name and password. The link expires in 48 hours; your seven days start when registration finishes.
  2. Sign in through Admin. Use the login page. Client and Vendor are for those account types.
  3. Open the sample residence. Expand Residences & Clients in the left menu, choose Residences, then open a residence card. Tap Menu first on smaller screens.
  4. Try the inspection. Open the residence’s Inspections tab. Explore a draft and mark findings Pass, Monitor or Attention.
  5. Create a practice work order. Use Work orders under Daily Operations, or publish a practice inspection and use its finding’s work-order action.
  6. Explore an arrival. Review Shopping and Arrivals in the residence. Check needs, preferred items and room preparation.

Need a clean start? Reset demo in your banner removes practice records and uploads, disables added accounts and restores sample data. Your login and expiry stay the same. Report emails and payments are disabled in the demo.

Read the quick-start transcript

01 · Activate your private demo

Welcome to your Estate Aegis demo. This quick start will help you find your way around in just a few minutes. Open the invitation in your email and choose Activate your demo. Enter your name and choose your own password. Your seven days begin when registration is complete. No credit card is required. Keep the quick-start link handy whenever you need a refresher.

02 · Choose the Admin portal

For later visits, open Estate Aegis dot com slash login and choose Admin. Client and Vendor are separate views for those account types. Use the email and password you registered with. Your demo is private to your company. The following screen examples use demonstration names; the sample names in your workspace may differ.

03 · Find your way around

Start on Overview. Expand Action needed to see what needs attention, or choose a dashboard tile to open its records. The left menu groups the main tools. On a smaller screen, tap Menu to reveal it. Residences and Clients is your starting point for families and homes. Daily Operations brings the company's work together.

04 · Open a sample residence

Open Residences, then choose a sample home's residence card. This is that home's dashboard. Its tabs keep inspections, shopping, arrivals and other records together. Overview shows upcoming work, recent inspections and family information. You can explore the prepared sample first, then try adding your own fictional family and residence. Create the family before adding its home.

05 · Try an inspection

Open the residence's Inspections tab and choose a draft, or start an inspection. Work through the checklist, using Pass, Monitor or Attention for each finding. Add a short note so the next person understands what you found. Save your draft as you go. In your private demo, report emails are disabled, so you can practice without emailing a real client.

06 · Turn a finding into work

To explore the repair workflow, complete and publish a practice inspection, then use its finding's work-order action. Review the title, description and due date before saving. The linked work order lets you track the issue through service and completion. You can also create a practice work order directly from Work orders in Daily Operations.

07 · Explore an arrival

Back in the residence, open Shopping to review preferred items. Then open Arrivals and explore the upcoming sample visit. Check the needs, groceries and room preparation so you can see what must be ready before the family arrives. These records give your team one place to coordinate the home's preparation.

08 · Reset, learn more and get help

Ready to start over? Use Reset demo in the banner. Confirm only when you are ready to remove your practice records and uploads and restore the sample. Your login stays the same, and resetting does not extend your seven days. The banner also links back to this guide.
