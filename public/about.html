About EstateAegis

Private residence care, thoughtfully organized.

EstateAegis was created around a simple idea: caring for private residences should feel organized, personal, and professional.

It brings inspections, arrivals, service work, and communication into one workspace—so teams can spend more time caring for homes.

Built around the home and the people who care for it.

Start with the family, connect its residences, and bring the rooms, preferences, inspections, and work into context. Administrators, clients, staff, and vendors use the information and workflows available to their roles.

Your company leads the experience.

Your company name and logo appear in the workspace and inspection reports. EstateAegis provides the software behind the experience, while your team maintains the relationship with the client.

See the workflow for yourself.

Start a free 7-day demo workspace with sample records. No credit card required. The tutorial shows how families, residences, inspections, arrivals, and service work connect.
