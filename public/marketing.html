What’s new in EstateAegis

Built for the way Florida home watch actually works.

Five new tools for the field and the office, from the first walkthrough to the week after a storm.

NEW01 / Checklists

Custom checklist templates.

Build your own visit checklists, or start from ready-made templates and adjust them to each home.

  • Starters for routine, arrival, departure, seasonal, maintenance, hurricane prep and post-storm visits
  • Mark items required, ask for a photo on fail, and choose which items alert the office
  • Field staff only see a checklist once you publish it
NEW02 / Mobile

Offline mobile inspections.

No signal at the gate or in the garage? Keep working. The inspection carries on and catches up later.

  • Checklists, notes and photos save on the phone
  • Everything syncs when you’re back online
  • A sync panel shows exactly what’s still waiting
NEW03 / Proof of visit

GPS proof of every visit.

Check in on arrival and the visit is verified at the residence, with the distance and GPS accuracy recorded.

  • Arrival, departure and time on site on every report
  • The same Visit verification box in the client portal
  • Location is recorded only at check-in, check-out and when photos are taken
NEW04 / Fail alerts

Fail alerts when something’s wrong.

When an inspection comes back with failed items, your administrators and the home’s residence manager get an email and an in-app alert.

  • Each alert lists the failed items and the inspector’s notes
  • One tap opens the inspection
NEW05 / Storms

Hurricane & storm workflow.

Run a storm from the first warning to the last post-storm check, and send each family an insurance-ready storm report.

  • Pre-storm and post-storm visits for every home, in bulk
  • Prep and damage status for each residence on one board

A few things to know

Built for your company’s daily work.

Can we use our own company branding?

Yes. Add your company name and logo in Company settings. Your branding appears in the workspace and on inspection reports, with Powered by EstateAegis in the report footer.

What can clients and vendors see?

Clients use a focused portal for their assigned residences, reports, arrivals, service requests, messages, and approvals. Vendors work with jobs assigned to their vendor account.

Can we try it before deciding?

Yes. Start a free 7-day demo workspace: verify your email, create your account and explore sample records. No credit card required.

What’s the difference between the free demo and the 30-day trial?

The free 7-day demo is a private practice workspace with temporary sample records and a reset button. No credit card is needed, and outgoing report emails and payments are turned off. The 30-day free trial is separate: it starts when you sign up for a paid plan, it’s your company’s real workspace, and checkout is through Stripe. Billing begins after the 30 days unless you cancel. The demo and your paid workspace are separate, so demo records don’t carry over.

What does it cost, and how do we get support?

Plans start at $59/month (launch pricing) and include 2 admin/staff users and 10 GB of storage. View pricing or contact Sales. Existing customers can reach software support at help@estateaegis.com.
