A few things to know

Built for your company’s daily work.

Can we use our own company branding?

Yes. Add your company name and logo in Company settings. Your branding appears in the workspace and on inspection reports, with Powered by EstateAegis in the report footer.

What can clients and vendors see?

Clients use a focused portal for their assigned residences, reports, arrivals, service requests, messages, and approvals. Vendors work with jobs assigned to their vendor account.

Can we try it before deciding?

Explore the private demo. Verify your email, create your account and explore sample records. No credit card required.

How do we discuss pricing and support?

Plans start at $59/month with launch pricing with two team accounts and 10 GB storage. View pricing or contact Sales. Existing users can reach software support at help@estateaegis.com.
