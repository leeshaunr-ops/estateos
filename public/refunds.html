BILLING

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Canceling

An authorized workspace administrator may cancel in the billing portal or by emailing help@estateaegis.com. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and normally keeps access available through the end of the current paid billing period.

Refunds

Monthly subscriptions and add-ons are not prorated or refunded for partial periods, unused seats, unused storage, nonuse or failure to cancel before renewal. EstateAegis will correct duplicate or erroneous charges and issue any refund required by applicable law. Stripe may display additional payment details during checkout.

Access and records

Cancellation does not automatically delete workspace records or files. Request an export or deletion separately through help@estateaegis.com. Payment failure may restrict new activity while existing records remain subject to the Terms and Privacy Policy.
