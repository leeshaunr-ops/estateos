[ ] Check accessible interior rooms for visible leaks, unusual odors, or damage.

[ ] Record observations for the agreed HVAC, plumbing, and appliance checks.

[ ] Photograph findings and classify items that need monitoring or attention.

[ ] Confirm the agreed closing and security steps before leaving.
