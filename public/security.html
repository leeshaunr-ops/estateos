Security

EstateAegis, LLC is committed to protecting the residence and household information entrusted to the service.

Access controls

Accounts require authentication, and workspace roles limit access to administrative, staff, vendor, and client functions.

Data handling

Residence records, photos, documents, access instructions, and messages are used to provide the service and are not used for unrelated advertising.

Responsible reporting

If you believe you found a security issue, contact help@estateaegis.com with enough detail for us to investigate.
