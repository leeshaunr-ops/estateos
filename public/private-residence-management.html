For estate managers and private home management companies

Private residence care starts with knowing the household: who is arriving, what they prefer, which spaces need preparation, and who is responsible for the home. EstateAegis connects that context to the inspections, schedules, and service work your team handles each day.

Start with the family

Create the family and its residences. Add family members with email addresses and manage their invitations and linked access from the residence.

Prepare for each arrival

Keep preferred shopping items, room readiness, guest details, and special instructions together so the team can prepare the home.

Coordinate your people

Assign work and staff schedules, keep vendor updates with the job, and use in-system messages for direct or company-wide communication.

Common questions

Know what to expect.

Do clients and vendors use the same screens as administrators?

They have role-specific portals. Clients can review the residence information and requests available to them; vendors can work with their assigned jobs.

How do we keep track of questions about work?

Add notes or request more information on the work order. Use messages when you need a separate conversation, with unread notifications in the sidebar.
